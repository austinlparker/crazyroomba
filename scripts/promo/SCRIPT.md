# Crazy Roomba · All Guts. No Dust.

30-second vertical commercial. The premise is a mundane household instruction taken absurdly far: dust becomes points, chores become stunts, and a tiny vacuum takes the whole thing to the Moon. The Daily challenge gives the viewer something to do next.

## Announcer

> They said, clean the house. You heard, let's get radical! Turn dust into points and chores into chaos! From the stairs to the streets... All the way to the freakin' moon! One tiny vacuum. Maximum attitude! Now grab your friends and show the Daily who's boss! Crazy Roomba! All guts. No dust. Play free!

The opening is dry and narrow, like a small TV speaker. “LET’S GET RADICAL” opens into full-band skate-punk and a raspy, gleefully excessive arcade-commercial announcer. A few short word echoes add size without obscuring the next sentence. Leave the gaps for music and action; do not fill them with more feature callouts.
